Questions

Frequently asked

The things renters ask us most. Anything else, give the office a call — (719) 596-2156.

What kind of apartments does Birchwood Village offer?

We offer one-bedroom apartment homes — all single-level ranch style, so there are no stairs and no neighbors overhead. Homes have wood vinyl plank flooring, ceiling fans and newer appliances with an overhead microwave. Renovated homes and air conditioning are available in select units.

Is Birchwood Village pet friendly?

Yes. Cats and dogs are welcome — up to two pets per apartment home, with no breed restrictions and no weight limit. There is a $350 pet fee and a $350 pet deposit per pet, plus $25–$35 in monthly pet rent. The community also has an on-site pet play area.

Does Birchwood Village accept housing vouchers?

Yes, we accept housing authority vouchers, and we do business in accordance with the Fair Housing Act. Call the office at (719) 596-2156 with any questions about the process.

Where is Birchwood Village located in Colorado Springs?

We’re at 3615–3618 E Uintah St., Colorado Springs, CO 80909, on the city’s east side — minutes from the Citadel Mall along with shopping, restaurants and entertainment. See the map →

What amenities are included?

A seasonal swimming pool, outdoor grilling area, expansive courtyards, a pet play area, on-site laundry facilities, on-site maintenance and management, and security monitoring of common areas. Birchwood Village is a smoke free property. See the full list →

Is parking available?

Yes. Off-street parking is available on the property for residents and guests at no additional cost. Assigned spaces and carports are not offered.

Are furnished apartments or corporate leases available?

Yes — furnished homes and corporate leases are both available. Call the office at (719) 596-2156 to talk through terms and current availability.

How do I check current pricing and availability?

Call (719) 596-2156, email contact.ppre@gmail.com, or view the current listing on Apartments.com. The office is open Tuesday–Friday 1:00pm–4:00pm and Saturday 11:00am–4:00pm, with Monday and Sunday by appointment.
