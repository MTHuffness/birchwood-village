Living here

A wonderful place to live

Every home is a single-level ranch — no stairs, no neighbors overhead. On-site laundry, on-site maintenance, on-site management and security monitoring of common areas.

  • Single-level living

    Ranch style one bedroom homes with wood vinyl plank flooring and ceiling fans throughout.

  • Pool & courtyards

    A seasonal swimming pool, BBQ grills and expansive courtyards to spread out in.

  • Minutes from everything

    Close to the Citadel Mall, grocery, restaurants, entertainment and major routes across the city.

  • A home for everyone

    We accept housing authority vouchers, and furnished homes and corporate leases are available.
